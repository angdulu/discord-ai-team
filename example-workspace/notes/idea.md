# Idea

**Pitch:** Snap your fridge, get three dinners you can cook tonight with what you already have.

- **Problem:** People throw away food they bought with good intentions, then order takeout because "there's nothing to cook."
- **Who:** Busy people cooking for 1–2, students, young couples.
- **Why now:** Phone cameras plus vision models can finally recognize groceries well enough.
- **Goal for the side project:** 500 weekly active users by the end of the beta, to learn whether people come back.

**Assumption:** People will take a fridge photo more than twice a week.
