# Target users

1. **Mina, 27, office worker, lives alone.** Buys vegetables on Sunday, throws half away by Friday. Wants: "something quick with what I have."
2. **Jae & Sora, 30, couple.** Argue about what to cook. Want: a shortlist they can both agree on in under a minute.
3. **Daniel, 20, student.** Tight budget, basic skills. Wants: cheap, 3-step recipes, no special equipment.

Shared pain: deciding takes longer than cooking.
