# Competitors

| App | What it does | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| SuperCook | Type in ingredients → recipes | Manual typing is tedious; people stop after a week |
| Samsung Food (Whisk) | Recipe saving, meal plans, shopping lists | Planning-first, not "what can I make right now" |
| Generic chatbots | Ask for a recipe from a list | No memory of your fridge; you retype every time |

**Our angle:** zero typing (photo in), remembers your pantry, three options max.
