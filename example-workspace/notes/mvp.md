# MVP scope (draft)

**In**
- Photo of fridge/shelf → detected ingredient list (editable)
- 3 recipe suggestions, ranked by "uses what expires first"
- "I cooked this" button → removes used ingredients
- Basic pantry list that persists

**Cut for now**
- Shopping lists
- Nutrition tracking
- Social sharing
- Barcode scanning

**Assumption:** Editing the detected list is acceptable if detection is ~80% right.
